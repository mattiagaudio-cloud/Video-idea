# TREATMENT — "GTA 6 gratis" (Microgame Frenzy, TikTok)

**Brief.** Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, ~26 s (fitted to the user's ElevenLabs voice track). Italian. Voice: the user's ElevenLabs file, used as is; word timings from faster-whisper. **No music** (the user adds a track in TikTok): the film ships voice + foley only, with headroom for the TikTok song. Subtitles burned in + `.srt`. On screen: "GTA 6" as plain text in the film's own lettering (no official logo, key art or music), "Buddy" as text only, code `B2601M2FCQ7OBC` (the character after the 7 is the letter O). No storyboard review.

TikTok safe area: nothing important in the bottom 420 px (caption, buttons) or the right 140 px (like/comment column) or the top 160 px.

## 1. Three structures

1. **Lives board as balance.** The home "lives" counter is a bank balance: 0 → 80,00 → 0,01 → +50 +50 +50. The number carries the story with no extra explaining.
2. **Relay.** Each game's result is the object handed into the next: the tap opens the app, the app takes the code, the code drops the coins, the coins pay the game, the leftover cent rolls out to the viewer.
3. **Rewind from a "GRATIS" receipt.** Open on the receipt, rewind through the games that led to it.

**Chosen: 2 + 1 together.** The relay gives the money a path that's easy to follow (one action per game, follow the coin), and the balance board, which stays on screen, is the scoreboard that makes it a game. Option 3 delays the hook, and TikTok gives us no time for that.

**Hero:** *Cent*, a one-cent copper coin with two dot eyes and a thick "1" on its face. It is what's left over at the end of the story, so the punchline is the hero itself. It keeps three marks in every medium: a round copper disc, the "1", and a little red sweatband.

**Home stage:** a deep violet set with a rotating sunburst. The frame-within-the-frame is a giant **smartphone** standing on the stage, the natural screen for TikTok and for a bank app. The balance board is a scoreboard above it, the stage badge sits on the left, and the trigger prop is a big red button below the phone.

## 2. Logline and arc

A one-cent coin plays its way through five microgames, from opening a Buddy account to buying GTA 6 for €79.99, and ends up being the change. Then it hands the game, and the code, to you.

- **Setup (0–3 s):** we're already inside the game: GTA 6, price tag €79.99, "GRATIS" slammed over it.
- **Turn (3–12 s):** how: APRI → CODICE → INCASSA (80,00) → PAGA (0,01, the cent rolls out).
- **Ending (12–26 s):** the cent turns to the viewer: TOCCA A TE, the code, SPEED UP, the invites (+50 ×3), LINK IN BIO. The balance board ends higher than it started, an echo of the opening "GRATIS".

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** fast party-game cold opens (one-second rules, slam-in verbs, frame-within-the-frame transitions) and the best short-form fintech explainers on TikTok (the number is always on screen and changes on the beat).
- **Don't take:** any party-game franchise's characters, UI or sounds, any Rockstar logo, key art, font or music, any Buddy/UniCredit marks.

## 5. Beat sheet

Grid: 140 BPM (one beat = 0.4286 s) for cuts and stamps, stepping up to 168 BPM at SPEED UP. Final times are moved onto the voice's word onsets: every command lands on the stressed syllable of its line.

## 6. Cue map (no music: the grid only drives cuts and foley)

- 0–12 s: 140 BPM. Characters on twos (15 fps); camera, transitions and the timer on ones.
- 12–16.8 s: a near-silent beat on the spotlight (only a heartbeat thump), then the code letters land one per eighth note.
- 16.8 s on: 168 BPM, characters on ones, games shorten to 1.6 s.

## 8. Type

- **Command words:** Titan One, white, a thick ink outline, a magenta drop, skewed −4°, slamming in over 3 frames, then shrinking into a corner tag (secondary ones fly out).
- **Subtitle cards:** Lilita One, ~54 px, rounded cards in the home palette, placed above the TikTok bottom zone (centre y ≈ 1380).
- **The code:** a monospaced face with a slashed zero, so the letter O and the zero can never be confused; letters tracked wide, on a white card with a hard shadow.
- **Inside games:** each medium's own type (CRT glyphs, Swiss grotesk, risograph stencil).
